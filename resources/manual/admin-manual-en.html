Sign in and access

Sign in with the username or email assigned to your staff account. Use Profile to manage your details and security settings. Sign out from the account menu when you finish.

The application is available in English, German, and Spanish. The language menu changes the interface language. This manual is in English. Dates use DD.MM.YYYY in all three interface languages.

  • Driver opens transport reports. The report shows the pickup, service dates, and permitted contact information.
  • Group Leader can read customer, season, availability, and booking details and work with issues.
  • Manager can manage customers, bookings, payments, service periods, and catalogs.
  • Admin can also manage lower-privilege staff accounts and review logs.
  • Super Admin administers the highest staff access level. Account rules still govern which changes can be made.

Customers

  1. Open Customers. Search by customer ID, a name component, email, or guardian phone.
  2. Use the view action to review contact details and the customer's bookings. The list shows the total matching customers and the active customers on the visible page.
  3. As a Manager or above, use Add customer or the edit action. Enter the birth date in DD.MM.YYYY and save.

Bookings and payments

Create or change a booking

  1. Open Booking. Select an existing customer or check New customer, then choose Continue to form.
  2. Choose the service and dates. The calendar marks full days and days already booked for this customer as unavailable. An eligible new date is added to the schedule when you save.
  3. Choose a pickup from the shared locations or the locations for the selected service. Enter the skill level and any card, equipment, or consent details relevant to the activity.
  4. Check the total in EUR and select Add. In Records, use Edit to change dates or options and Cancel booking to release the places. Cancellation preserves the booking and its payment history.

Apply a payment

Enter the amount in EUR and choose Apply in Booking records, or choose Unpaid fees on Customers and use Pay on the matching booking. Verify the updated balance before entering another payment. The payment action rejects an amount above the remaining balance.

Services and seasons

  • Services shows one entry per service and season. Search by service or season name and filter by service, date, or status.
  • Availability shows the booked and remaining places for each scheduled date. Open the booking list for that date to review active and cancelled reservations. Use Reports to open the selected date or season range.
  • Managers can create and edit service periods. Periods for the same service must have separate date ranges. Historical booking dates must be retained.
  • A season uses its own daily capacity and the price tiers configured for the service. An absent rate displays Price not configured.
  • Seasons provides season summaries. Locations distinguishes shared pickup points from service-specific ones. Cards, Equipment, and Bonus services manage the corresponding catalogs.

Dashboard and reports

  1. Use the dashboard to check season totals and today's records. Historical bookings remain in the season totals.
  2. Open Reports, select a service and date range, and click Generate. The report header identifies the generated range. Fields edited afterwards are applied when you generate again.
  3. Header filters refine the visible page. The page CSV exports the visible rows. CSV (All) and Print (All) use the generated service and date range across pages.
  4. Driver reports show permitted transport information. Financial and other restricted customer columns are available only to the relevant staff roles.

Staff administration and issues

  • Use Users to create staff accounts within your role's authority. Save the account changes and ask the staff member to sign in using their assigned credentials.
  • Use Logs to review recorded events. The current role determines available actions.
  • Use Issues to report a problem and follow its status. Include the module and the steps that led to the issue. Attach files only when they are needed for the investigation.

Troubleshooting

A booking date or pickup is unavailable Check the service and date in Services. Then check the selected customer's existing reservations and the pickup's service assignment.
A report is empty Confirm the generated service and dates. Clear the visible-page filters or select All services and generate again.
An action is unavailable Check the role in Profile. Ask an authorized staff member to perform the action if your role is read-only for that module.
