Sign in and get oriented

Start with your assigned staff account. Use the address provided by your organization and stay on the same address when signing in or using a passkey.

Start your shift

  1. Enter your username or email and password, then choose Log in. Complete any two-factor challenge that appears.
  2. Check the page you land on. Most staff open Home. Drivers open Reports.
  3. Choose EN, DE, or ES in the top bar. On a narrow screen, use Menu for module navigation and account actions.
  4. Open Manual in the top bar or mobile menu. Use the contents or search this page with the browser's Find command.
Expected result

The menu reflects your access. The interface and manual share the selected language. Dates appear as day.month.year in all three languages.

Work safely

  1. Search before creating a customer or booking. Use the exact dates, pickup point, and customer you intend.
  2. Submit a form once and wait for the result. After an error, check whether a row was saved before trying again.
  3. Keep credentials out of notes and issue attachments. Use Logout at the end of the shift and wait for the login page.
Business rule

Use the details in Roles and permissions when a control is missing and Troubleshooting when an action fails.

Dashboard and statistics

Home summarizes the operating season and shows a list for today. Group Leaders and above can use it. Drivers are directed to the report view.

Read the season cards

  1. Confirm the displayed season dates before comparing totals. The configured start and end dates under Seasons determine the operating-season window.
  2. Read the season records, unique clients, scheduled dates, and the total, paid, and due values. A multi-day booking counts once in the season booking and payment totals.
  3. Compare the per-service breakdown to identify which activities have scheduled dates and bookings. The overall customer line shows active customers out of all customers.

Plan today's work

  1. Use today's entries to review customers, activities, pickup points, and contacts. When there are more than 100 entries, use Reports for the complete date range.
  2. Open Services → Availability for the activity and date before promising a place. Switch to the customer or booking record to confirm an individual balance.
Expected result

After a booking or payment, revisit the dashboard to check the refreshed totals. Clear differences between a calendar date, a booking count, and a customer count before reporting numbers.

Business rule

The dashboard preserves historical booking records in its aggregates. Use active booked and remaining places in daily availability when making capacity decisions.

Customers

Keep one record for each customer. Contact and birth details support booking, transport, and report workflows.

Find and review a customer

  1. Open Customers, enter an ID, name or name component, email, or guardian phone in the search field, then apply the filter.
  2. Open the matching row's view action and check their name, birth date, contact details, and bookings before using the record.
  3. If there are several matches, compare birth date and guardian contact. Clear the filter to return to the full list. The results counter covers all matching customers. The active counter covers the current page.

Add or change a record

  1. As a Manager or above, choose Add customer or use the edit action on the matching row.
  2. Enter the first and last names. Use the separate name field where applicable. Enter the birth date as DD.MM.YYYY and check the email and guardian phone.
  3. Set the active checkbox as appropriate, save, then find the record again. Check the birth date and contact details before returning to Booking.
Expected result

If a booking is already attached, use the customer's details and booking records when reviewing its dates and balance.

Business rule

Use Unpaid fees to review outstanding bookings. Group Leaders can review the list. Payment controls require Manager access or higher.

Services and service periods

The Services screen shows separate entries for each service and its configured periods. One service can appear several times with different date ranges and capacities.

Find the relevant service period

  1. Search by service or season name, or choose a service and a date within its period.
  2. Use the status and sort controls to compare active, upcoming, and expired periods. An unscheduled service has no configured period.
  3. Check the dates, daily capacity, and price tiers shown on the service-period row. Open Availability for a specific day or Reports for the period range.

Maintain services and rates

  1. As a Manager or above, use Add service where available or the service catalog's edit controls. Set the service name and its available translations. Review its capacity and active state.
  2. Review the service duration-price tiers in the service tools. Choose the tier that matches the booked duration. Investigate Price not configured before quoting a standard rate.
  3. Use the period actions to open the schedule for the relevant service. Keep the service catalog, dated capacity, and seasonal defaults aligned before taking bookings.
Expected result

One service definition can support several periods. A new period for an existing service keeps the same service in the calendar, locations, and reports.

Business rule

Filtering by a date within a period finds the period covering that date. Use its daily availability view to check whether that specific date is scheduled.

Daily availability and capacity

The service period sets the daily capacity. Active bookings on a specific date consume places. Cancelling a booking releases its places and retains the booking history.

Check a day and its bookings

  1. In Services, find the service and period. Open Availability.
  2. Filter to the intended date or review all scheduled dates. Read Booked places and the remaining places on the matching row.
  3. Open the day's booking list to see active and cancelled records. Use the day's report action for transport or other day-specific work.

Create or edit a service period

  1. As a Manager or above, open the period creation or edit action for the service. Select the service if the form asks for it, enter a period name and daily capacity, and select the intended calendar dates.
  2. Recheck the first and last date and the days actually selected. Periods for one service need separate date ranges even if some dates inside a range have no slot.
  3. Save and reopen Availability. Check the schedule and capacity before telling staff that the period is ready.
Expected result

In the demo, a day with capacity 12 and three active bookings has nine free places. A new booking for this day and a second day reduces each day’s remaining capacity by one. Check both dates after saving.

Business rule

Retain linked historical dates. Edits that remove dates with booking history or lower capacity below the active bookings are rejected. The server checks availability again when a booking is saved.

Season archive and annual defaults

Use the archive for a summary of service periods and historical records. The annual season defaults set the recurring operating window used by the dashboard.

Review a period

  1. Open Seasons and choose the relevant past or current period. Review its service, date range, and figures.
  2. Follow the booking or report context for that period when available. Use the same service and dates when comparing with a generated report.
  3. For today's free places, return to the Services row and its daily Availability.

Plan a new season

  1. As a Manager or above, open Seasons and select the service tab. Edit Default bounds for new season using day-month values, for example 01-09. Select the service to use Create season or edit a selected season by its dates.
  2. Before changing defaults, confirm the dates with the team. Defaults affect the operating window. Each service still needs the relevant periods and dates.
  3. Open the service period after saving. Confirm the visible date range and capacity before scheduling customers.
Expected result

Seasonal summaries and booking availability answer different questions. Use the archive for history and the daily view to confirm bookable capacity.

Pickup and drop-off locations

A shared location can be selected by any service. A service-specific location belongs to the service chosen in the form.

Create or edit a pickup route

  1. As a Manager or above, open Locations and choose Add location, or edit the existing route.
  2. Enter pickup and drop-off names and their times. Review the direction of the route and the two times before saving.
  3. Choose Main (all services) for a shared pickup point, or choose a service for an activity-specific point. Save and check the appropriate group.

Verify the booking and report views

  1. Open a Booking for the relevant service. Check that its location list contains the expected shared and service-specific choices.
  2. If a pickup is missing, recheck the booking's selected service and the location's assignment. Choose the correct scope based on where the route should be used.
  3. For a transport run, generate a dated report and confirm the displayed route and time before printing or exporting.
Expected result

A shared route appears in the main group when at least one exists. A service-specific route appears with its service.

Create a booking

Start with the customer, activity, intended dates, pickup point, and payment details. The Booking workspace is available to Managers and higher roles.

Choose the customer and activity

  1. Open Booking. For an existing customer, search by name and select the matching suggestion. Check the contact and birth details after continuing.
  2. If the customer has no existing record, select New customer. Choose the booking type and select Continue to form, then enter the required name and other details.
  3. Select the service or additional activity that was agreed. For a dated service, choose the correct pickup from the shared points or the points for that service.

Select days, options, and amounts

  1. Select each intended day in the calendar. You can choose several dates for the same service in one booking. Check the selected-date list, including the month and year.
  2. Full dates and dates already booked for this customer and service are unavailable. An eligible date can be added to the schedule when the booking is saved.
  3. For a ski service, choose the skill level. Add a card, equipment and any requested measurements when applicable. Mark parental consent only when it has been received. Add a note if staff need it.
  4. Confirm the total in EUR, and enter an initial paid amount only if payment was actually received. Choose Add once and wait for the result.
Expected result

The booking appears in Records. The selected customer's details and the corresponding daily availability reflect the saved booking. Each selected day uses one place.

Business rule

Additional activities use their own catalog context. A dated service booking is the workflow for selecting service slots and reserving places on several days.

Edit or cancel a booking

Use Records on the Booking page to find a saved reservation. Search when the list spans several pages. Review the selected activity and dates before changing anything.

Edit a saved booking

  1. Find the matching customer and row. Choose Edit and check the existing dates, pickup, options, and balance.
  2. Toggle calendar dates to add or remove days. Check the completed set of dates before saving. Change the relevant options or note and review the total.
  3. Leave payment fields alone for an ordinary edit. If a payment correction is needed, verify the latest balance and follow the payment instructions.
  4. Save, reopen the record, and compare its itinerary with the activity's daily availability. Check both the days added and the days removed.

Cancel a booking

  1. Confirm the customer, service, dates, and payment history before selecting Cancel booking. Confirm when prompted.
  2. Check the row's cancelled status and reopen Availability for a previously booked day. The released place should appear in the remaining capacity.
  3. Check the booking history to confirm the record is still available for review. Handle any refund separately under your organization’s procedures.
Business rule

Cancellation releases capacity and preserves the booking and payments. Use an edit when only one day changes.

Payments and outstanding balances

Compare the agreed total, paid amount, and amount due. A positive due amount is outstanding. A partial payment reduces the balance, and a settled record has no remaining amount.

Apply a payment

  1. Open Customers → Unpaid fees or find the booking in Booking → Records. Match the customer, activity, and booking before entering money.
  2. Enter the amount received in EUR. Choose Pay in the unpaid list or Apply in Records, then wait for the result.
  3. Check the updated paid and due amounts. A fully settled booking leaves the unpaid list. For a partial payment, check the smaller balance before applying any further payment.

Review a correction

  1. If a balance needs correcting, reopen the latest booking first. Use the editable paid amount in the booking form only for an authorized correction.
  2. Compare the entered value and the agreed total. The application rejects a payment above the remaining balance and rejects edits based on stale paid values.
  3. Reopen the booking after the change to confirm the stored balance. Compare the due amount before asking for another payment.
Expected result

For example, a partly paid booking remains visible in the unpaid view. Once the full balance is recorded, the record remains in Booking history with no amount due.

Business rule

Enter the amount taken in EUR and use the displayed balance for reconciliation.

Equipment, cards, and additional activities

Maintain the catalogs first, then pick their entries in the appropriate booking. Keep the service link and the duration-price values consistent with what staff quote.

Maintain equipment and cards

  1. Open Equipment for rentals and Cards for passes. As a Manager or above, add or edit the relevant item.
  2. Enter the name and supported translations, then link it to the service that will use it.
  3. Add the price rows for the intended numbers of days and enter each EUR price. Save and verify the service and prices in the list.
  4. In a ski booking, enable the card or equipment option as applicable and pick the correct item. Enter measurements when needed. Check the booking total before saving.

Maintain additional activities

  1. Open Bonus to create or edit a separate activity. Check its title, validity dates, route or meeting details, description, and any base or duration prices.
  2. Save and open the Booking workflow for an additional activity. Verify the correct entry and amount before saving the booking.
  3. Review the additional-activity list and booking record when checking registrations. Use the dated service reports for transport runs connected to service slots.
Business rule

If an item is missing in Booking, check whether you chose the correct service and whether the catalog item is linked to it. Review existing records before removing a catalog item.

Generate, filter, print, and export reports

Reports show booking records for a selected service and date range. Driver accounts open this module directly and see the transport information allowed for that role.

Generate and review a report

  1. Select a service or All services, enter the start and end dates, and choose Generate. For a single day, use the same start and end date.
  2. Confirm the service and dates in the generated report header. Inspect the customer rows, pickup times, and any role-permitted totals.
  3. Use the column filters to refine the visible page. If the report is empty, clear those filters and confirm the generated range before choosing another service.

Export the intended rows

  1. Use CSV (Page) or Print (Page) for the current page, including its visible filters. Review the download or print preview before sharing.
  2. Use CSV (All) or Print (All) for all pages of the generated service and date range. Generate again after editing the input fields.
  3. Check the result's audience before sharing. Drivers see permitted transport fields such as pickup, dates, and contact. Financial data and other restricted customer fields depend on role.
Expected result

Editing a draft date or service leaves the generated header and export range in place until you choose Generate again.

Business rule

For a whole-range export, use the generated service and dates as the scope. Header filters operate on the current visible page.

Staff accounts and activity logs

Use Users to maintain staff access. Administrator and Super Admin accounts have different visibility over staff and available roles.

Add or update a staff account

  1. Open Users. Search by staff identity and confirm whether that person already has an account.
  2. Choose Add user or the edit action. Enter the account details, choose an allowed role, set the active state, and use the password fields when setting or changing a password.
  3. Save once. Check the row and role again. Ask the staff member to sign in using the assigned credentials through a secure channel.
  4. To revoke access, use the authorized account controls. Credential, role, and active-state changes can end existing access. Review the staff member's responsibilities before changing their role.

Review activity logs

  1. Open Logs if your role provides access. Use the entries to check when an action occurred and which account or page was involved.
  2. If entries are missing, check your access. Administrators do not see their own entries in this view. Super Admin can review all entries.
  3. Follow your organization's rules before deleting log entries. Confirm the scope if the screen offers selected or bulk deletion.
Business rule

Administrator role assignment and actions are limited by the account policy. A higher-rank account such as Super Admin remains outside an Administrator's authority. Staff public registration is disabled.

Roles and permissions

Check Profile to confirm your role. Use the available controls and ask an authorized colleague for operations outside your role.

Operational roles

  1. Driver can open Reports for transport work. Use the allowed pickup, service, date, and contact fields.
  2. Group Leader can review the dashboard, customers, unpaid records, services, seasons, and daily bookings, and use Issues within the allowed scope. Customer writes, booking creation, and payment controls require Manager access.
  3. Manager can maintain customers, create, edit or cancel bookings, record payments, manage service schedules and catalogs, and review reports.

Administrative roles

  1. Administrator has the operational permissions plus eligible staff administration and log access. Account actions are limited to identities and active roles within that administrator's authority.
  2. Super Admin has the highest staff level and, when configured, access to Horizon for queue monitoring. Per-account and system rules still apply.
Expected result

A Driver logging in lands in Reports. A Group Leader reviewing the same customer can read the record, while a Manager can act on it.

Business rule

A missing write action is often a role boundary. If the expected role differs from the Profile label, ask an administrator to review the account.

Report and track an issue

Use Issues to record a problem that needs follow-up. Report the symptom, the module, and the steps needed to reproduce it.

Create a useful issue

  1. In New report, select the module, issue type, and priority. Enter a short title and describe what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
  2. Include the service or visible record identifier when needed. Provide the page path if it helps a colleague find the problem.
  3. Add an attachment only when it helps explain the issue. Remove private data and credentials from screenshots. Submit and locate the new issue in the list.

Follow progress

  1. Filter the list by status or module when needed. Open the issue to read its details and comments.
  2. Add a comment to update colleagues. The author and a Super Admin can also change status or assignee. Choose the appropriate state, such as open, in progress, or resolved, and verify the saved result.
  3. Return to Issues to check the outcome and read replies.
Expected result

A reproducible record lets another staff member verify the exact problem and follow its resolution.

Profile and application settings

The top bar or mobile menu opens Profile. Review your personal details there and confirm the displayed role before doing privileged work.

Update profile or password

  1. Open Profile and review the editable details. Save any correction and check the displayed name and email.
  2. Use the password settings link to enter the current password, the new password, and confirmation. Save and keep the new credential private.
  3. Use Settings → Appearance to choose Light, Dark, or System where available. The active theme may follow your device setting when System is selected.

Set up sign-in protection

  1. For a passkey, enter an optional device name in the Profile passkey panel and choose Add passkey. Confirm your password if asked and follow the browser or device prompt. Sign in later from the same host used for enrollment.
  2. For a two-factor code, use the account's two-factor settings, follow the authenticator setup, and complete verification. Store recovery codes securely and keep them outside shared notes or screenshots.
  3. If you lose access, use the recovery process offered at login or contact your administrator. Report the step that failed without sharing a credential or recovery code.
Business rule

Your browser, operating system, and sign-in address affect passkey availability. Use the same authorized URL in daily work.

Shift and recurring checklists

Use these checks to tie the individual modules together and catch gaps before they affect the next shift.

Before a shift or handover

  1. Check the current dashboard and generate today's report if needed. Confirm service, dates, pickup times, and the role appropriate for the people receiving it.
  2. Use Services → Availability for planned sessions and review notes, consent, equipment, or cards in the relevant bookings.
  3. Apply confirmed payments and verify the outstanding balances. Log problems in Issues. Sign out at the end of the shift.

Weekly or before a season changes

  1. Review service periods, available dates, daily capacity, annual defaults, and old bookings in the archive.
  2. Confirm prices and service links for cards, equipment, and additional activities. Review shared and service-specific pickup routes for duplicates or outdated times.
  3. If you administer staff, review active accounts and role assignments. Revoke access when appropriate and follow the local log retention rules.
Expected result

A colleague can reproduce the reports and find the current work from the service, date, and booking details.

Troubleshooting

Open the item matching the symptom. Check the on-screen result before repeating a write.

I cannot sign in

Check the address, username or email, password, and Caps Lock. Complete the two-factor challenge if prompted. Use the reset link or ask an administrator to check your access.

A booking date or pickup is unavailable

Check that the service and dates agree with Services → Availability. A full day or an existing active booking for the same customer and service can block a date. For a missing pickup, compare the location's scope and the chosen service.

A service period overlaps another one

Review all periods for the same service, including expired and upcoming entries. Use a date range that does not intersect another period, and keep dates with existing booking links.

A payment is rejected or the balance looks wrong

Reopen the latest booking and check the EUR value and current balance. An amount over the balance is rejected. For a stale correction, reload and recheck before making it again.

A report is empty

Check the service and dates printed in the generated header. Clear current-page column filters or generate again with All services and the correct range.

An action is unavailable

Check the staff level in Profile. If your role has read access only, ask an authorized staff member to complete the change. Drivers use the report module.

The screen language or dates look unfamiliar

Select the language from the top bar or this manual's language links. Dates use day.month.year. Service catalog names can show their configured fallback language when a translation is missing.

The page is slow or a save seems stuck

Wait for the current request and inspect the result. After it settles, revisit the relevant list before submitting again. If the problem persists, open an issue with the module and steps.
